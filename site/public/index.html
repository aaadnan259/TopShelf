How it works

1

Reads what's on the page

Nothing else. Every rating, review count, and price is already on your screen. No servers, no accounts, no requests.

2

Scores every listing

A confidence-weighted rating pulls thin-review products toward the page average until they've earned their spot.

3

You stay in control

One tap back to Amazon's order. Every rank explains itself in a click. Nothing is ever deleted — only reordered.

What's inside

Quality & Value modes

Rank by confidence-weighted rating, or by quality per dollar (log-scaled).

Review-farm demotion

High ratings on suspiciously few reviews get pushed down — never hidden on that alone.

Sponsored, handled

Demoted by default. Hide them entirely or leave them alone — your call.

Explainable ranks

Click any badge to see the exact numbers and penalties behind the score.

Pairs with Knockoff

Reads Knockoff's brand verdicts when present. Not installed? Works the same.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, zero network requests. Settings stay in Chrome sync.
